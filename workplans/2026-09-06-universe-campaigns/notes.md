# Campaign investigation

The merged baseline exposes init/run/status/archive and optional local-model
generation. It records fixed-evaluator outcomes, archive lineage and reported
generation tokens. Run ownership and interrupted recovery exist, but there is
no campaign owner, campaign-wide envelope or automatic outcome feedback.

Prior actual local-model experiments produced three rejected candidates. Their
results must guide the next capability without weakening the evaluator or
relabeling failed artifacts as accepted engineering work.
