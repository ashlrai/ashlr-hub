# Ashlrverse visual assets

`public/ashlrverse-orbital.png` is original concept artwork created with the
built-in image-generation tool for this project on 2026-09-09. It is 1672 by 941
pixels, inspected before integration, and used as decorative hero imagery.
No third-party image was copied into this hero, and no model/provider execution is depicted as
real. The graph and bars, separately, use the recorded public evidence JSON.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: original cinematic in-page website hero background for Ashlrverse,
an open-source agent-native engineering fleet.

Primary request: Exactly one wide landscape 16:9 image of a restrained visionary
space observatory: a vast abstract orbital engineering habitat/ring occupies the
RIGHT half of the composition, set against a very dark ink-black/navy cosmos.
Realistic brushed titanium edges, a thin warm solar rim, restrained electric
ice-blue illumination, luminous distant stellar dust.

Style/medium: Sophisticated cinematic aerospace concept art with realistic
material detail; spacious, ambitious, contemplative, a frontier for builders.

Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape. Huge quiet dark negative space across the
LEFT half for separately rendered typography. Structure visually concentrated
on the RIGHT, subtly cut by the right edge, with atmospheric depth and immense
scale. Keep left half near-black and low contrast, sparse tiny stars only.
Full-bleed single scene.

Lighting/mood: Restrained warm solar backlight traces a thin titanium silhouette;
small ice-blue engineering lights, dim faraway stellar dust. Deep blacks
dominate; refined contrast and natural cinematic light.

Constraints: No Earth globe, no cartoon rockets, no weapons or warfare. No text,
letters, logos, UI, diagrams, badges, watermark, border, or collage. Generate
exactly one original asset.
