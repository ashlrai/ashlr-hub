# P07 — Durable signed allocation evidence
Status: implemented locally; parent integration pending; no runtime activation.
API: recordValueAllocation({root, allocationId, receipt, trace}, keyOptions) and readValueAllocations({root}, keyOptions).
Records retain the already-created P06 receipt and trace unchanged; a domain-separated HMAC binds their explicit allocation ID.
Default signing and verification use the existing provenance key read-only; inert test keys remain explicit.
An owned outer lock protects 256-record, 16 MiB aggregate and 1 MiB individual serialized-byte admission before publication.
Reads validate the whole bounded store; corrupt, incomplete, busy or unauthenticated evidence is unavailable without repair.
Exact replay is idempotent; different signed content conflicts; unknown ownership and pre-existing staging are refused.
Allocation IDs do not prevent capacity reuse; no leases, dispatch, budget renewal, providers or receipt regeneration are introduced.
Verification: 18 store tests and 88 combined tests, full typecheck, scoped ESLint and diff checks pass; byte-overflow seam is synthetic.
